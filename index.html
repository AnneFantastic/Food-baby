Longevity made easy

What we eat every day is one of the biggest things we can control for a long, healthy life. Eating mostly plants, with plenty of vegetables, fruit, beans, lentils, whole grains, nuts and seeds, is linked with lower risks of heart disease, type 2 diabetes and some cancers. The only real obstacle is planning. This page makes vegan and vegetarian eating the easy, obvious choice rather than the alternative: find recipes from what's already in your kitchen, plan your day or week, check the nutrition, and shop for the rest.

🌈AntioxidantsColourful plants are rich in polyphenols and carotenoids that help protect cells.
🌾FibreMost people fall short, and it only comes from plants. It feeds your gut and steadies blood sugar.
🍊Vitamins & mineralsVitamin C, folate, potassium, magnesium and iron, plus plant protein, in every meal.

One honest note: a vegan diet needs a reliable vitamin B12 source (a supplement or fortified foods), and iron and omega-3 are worth watching. The nutrition panel helps with this.
